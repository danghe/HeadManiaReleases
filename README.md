To download the latest version of the UpSampler for Windows 10/11:
1. Navigate to the **[Releases](../../releases)** page on the right side of this screen.
2. Download the `.zip` or `.exe` file under the **Assets** dropdown of the latest release.

## 🎵 About the Project
Built from the ground up for extreme audio performance, the HeadMania UpSampler bypasses OS audio mixers to deliver bit-perfect, 1-Million tap FIR filter upsampling. 
**Core Features:**
- **System-Wide Processing:** Capture audio from any application (Deezer, Spotify, YouTube, Roon) via WASAPI Loopback.
- **Extreme Mathematics:** Features our custom 1M-tap Hybrid (Linear/Minimum Phase) filters for organic tonality and explosive transients.
- **UPnP Network Streaming:** Acts as a headless server, allowing you to route 1M-tap upsampled audio directly to your high-end UPnP DAC or Streamer.
- **Audiophile DSP:** Built-in True Peak limiting (-1dB headroom), Harman AutoEQ profiles, and psychoacoustic enhancements (Zero Hard Clipping).
- **Hardware Synergy Engine:** Automatically tailors the digital output stage specifically for your physical DAC topology (Discrete R2R, Pure 1-Bit Native DSD, or Commercial Delta-Sigma) with optimized quantization and true-peak modulator ceilings.
- **Kinetic Transient Engine:** Shatters the "long-filter transient smearing" paradox. It actively monitors the audio envelope and dynamically shifts convolution weighting to a lightning-fast cascade filter the exact microsecond a drum impact occurs. You get explosive slam combined with the immense 1-Million tap soundstage.
- **O(1) Zero-Latency Streaming Core:** Built on lock-free ring buffers for absolutely flawless, stutter-free audio delivery even under extreme CPU loads.
